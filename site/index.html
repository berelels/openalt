02 · How it works

Three steps. Once.

1

Install on your PC

OpenAlt sets up Sunshine with its own config — yours stays untouched. Linux today, Windows next.

2

Turn it on

Nothing runs until you press the button. Press it again and everything goes back to how it was.

3

Scan the QR

Open the OpenAlt app on the phone and point it at the code. It works once and expires in two minutes. No PIN.
